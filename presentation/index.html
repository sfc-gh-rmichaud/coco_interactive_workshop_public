Highlight mode: press H, then simply move the pointer over any diagram block or card. Whatever is under the cursor stays lit and everything else on the slide dims, so the room knows exactly where to look. Move off, and the slide returns to normal. Nothing to click and nothing to reset — press H again, or Esc, to disarm it.
